Day of the Deb — information still needed for the website

The site is live and working. These are the blanks. Anything not filled in currently shows as “To be announced” on the page, so nothing looks broken while we wait — but every item below is something a guest will look for.

Most urgent

These three shape whether guests can plan at all.

  1. Start time — and end time, if you want one shown.
  2. RSVP deadline — the date guests must respond by. This appears at the top of the RSVP page and again on their confirmation screen, so it is the single most useful thing for getting responses in.
  3. The five honorees’ names, and how each should be spelled and ordered. The site does not list names yet; the lotería cards stand in for them. If you would rather names never appear in text, that is fine — just say so and we will stop asking.

Home page

  1. Photos. The gallery currently uses stock River Walk photos from a free stock library, clearly marked as placeholders. Replace them with your own whenever you have them — six or so works well.
  2. Lotería card artwork. We know the designer is producing these. No action needed until they arrive.

Venue page

  1. About the venue — two or three sentences describing Mexico Ceaty. What guests should expect walking in.
  2. Which entrance to use. Guests reach the river level through The Shops at Rivercenter — is there a specific door, escalator, or landmark they should head for once inside?
  3. Getting a ride home. Where should guests wait for an Uber or Lyft at the end of the night? This is the one people ask about at 11pm, and right now the site says nothing.
  4. Valet — is it offered? If yes, where, and what does it cost?
  5. Walking directions from the drop-off. We have the three broad steps from the invitation. If there is a clearer way to describe the last stretch — “past the fountain,” “down the escalator by the food court” — it helps.
  6. The step-free route. How does someone using a wheelchair, a walker, or a stroller get from the street down to the river level? Which elevator, and where is it? Please treat this as important rather than optional — it is the difference between some guests coming and not.
  7. Parking cost, if you would like it listed — hourly or event rate for either garage.
  8. The nearby event. The site currently warns guests that a large event is expected downtown that day and to allow extra time. If you can confirm what it is and how it affects traffic or street closures, we will make that specific. If it turns out not to be happening, we will remove the warning.
